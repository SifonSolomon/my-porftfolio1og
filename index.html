A Little About Me

The Creative Behind The Frames.

Hello! I'm Sifon Solomon, a passionate video editor and motion graphics designer based in Lagos, Nigeria. With a keen eye for detail and a love for storytelling, I specialize in transforming ideas into engaging visual experiences.

My work spans documentary editing, explainer videos, brand campaigns, commercials and logo animations. I enjoy finding the right balance between creative visuals and clear communication.

For me, every frame has a purpose. Whether I'm shaping a story through editing or adding depth through motion graphics, my goal is to create work that looks good and communicates effectively.
